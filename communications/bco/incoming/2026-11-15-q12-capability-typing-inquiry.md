# Three questions about capability-typed `bpo:P…` classes and three PCF elements with no act class

Derived in part from the APQC Process Classification Framework® 7.4 (element names and IDs,
under APQC's notice in BPO's `NOTICE.md`). APQC does not endorse this work.

## Why we are asking

A BCO capability type takes its realization index from BPO act classes. The BCO's rules
(specification v0.5.0-draft.3, CAP-03) do not allow an index member that is a class under
`cco:ont00000568` (Organization Capability). Ten of the BCO's finance concepts are
therefore blocked: eight have no act evidence except a capability-typed `bpo:P…` class,
and two lack an act class of their own. The BCO's architect decided on 2026-11-15 to ask
you, rather than retype anything or invent an act genus. The BCO's account of these
dependencies is `docs/catalog/tranche-2-q12.md` (in the refs).

This does not block the BCO's other work. The 42 capability types the BCO has admitted
take their indexes from classes you type as acts, and none of them waits for an answer.

## What we read, and at which commit

Everything below is read at your commit `bc64e91839378e1926189c01bf184d6a22d5c1b7`, which
the BCO pins. It was checked again at your `main` on 2026-11-15 (`14a1e2c`): the same 39
classes are typed under `cco:ont00000568` there, and no class `bpo:P10735`, `bpo:P10736`
or `bpo:P14008` is declared.

Each of the 39 carries an `rdfs:comment` that applies your ADR-001 rule R5 (the
bounded-occurrent test) and classifies the node as a standing function, and 33 also name
R6. Five record that they were re-modeled at Gate F, from an act to a capability. So we
read the typing as deliberate. We are asking you to confirm that reading. We are not
reporting an error.

## The questions

1. **Are the 39 `bpo:P…` classes below intended as capabilities, as their comments say,
   or as process (act) types?**
2. **For any of them intended as act types, which existing BPO act genus applies to each?**
   We do not propose a genus, and we do not assume Planned Act.
3. **Are act-type declarations intended for PCF elements 10735, 10736 and 14008?**

That class (line 256) is under `cco:ont00000568`. Its `bpo:enablesProcess` names `bpo:P10762`, `bpo:P10764` and `bpo:P21574` (`ontology/capabilities_wiring.ttl` lines 718–720) |

## What happens with your answer

- **If a class is a capability as intended,** the BCO keeps the concepts that depend on it
  blocked. It then looks for act types elsewhere in BPO, one at a time, under its own review.
- **If a class is an act type, and you assign it a genus,** the BCO re-pins to the commit
  that declares it. Each dependent concept then goes through the BCO's own G1-T admission
  review.
- **Either way,** nothing in BPO changes on the BCO's account, and the BCO retypes nothing.
