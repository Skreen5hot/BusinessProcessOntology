# BPO v0.4.1: prefix label `ex:` → `bpo:`

This is the rename the v0.4.0 notice said would follow. **No IRI changed.** `bpo:` abbreviates
`https://fandaws.com/ontology/bpo#`, the same namespace `ex:` did at v0.4.0.

## What it means for the BCO

- **References by full IRI are unaffected.** The six genera keep the IRIs given in the v0.4.0
  notice.
- **Your pin record says `prefix: ex`** (`config/upstream-sources.yaml`, id `bpo`). BPO's
  Turtle now declares `bpo:`, so if anything of yours reads or emits that label, now is the
  time to change it.
- **`apqc-ext.ttl` changed bytes but not meaning.** At `dda9fa5` its SHA-256 is
  `a8119728…6fd`. Compared with `327ea2c`, the graph differs only in `owl:versionInfo`
  (`0.4.1`) and in `ex:` → `bpo:` inside `rdfs:comment` text. Re-pinning to `327ea2c` or to
  `dda9fa5` gives the same terms, and `dda9fa5` is the current one.

Nothing here requires a reply.
